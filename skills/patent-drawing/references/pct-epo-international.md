# PCT, EPO, and Multi-Jurisdiction Drawing Requirements

When the application may enter the national phase in multiple countries or is filed as a PCT application, prepare drawings to the strictest common standard so they are accepted without rework.

## PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty) — Rule 11 and Article 7
- Drawings are required when necessary for understanding the invention; otherwise optional (designated Offices may later require them).
- Sheet size for the record copy: **A4 only** (21.0 × 29.7 cm). Receiving Offices may temporarily accept other sizes, but the record copy transmitted to the International Bureau must be A4.
- Usable surface (sight): ≤ 26.2 cm × 17.0 cm.
- Minimum margins: top 2.5 cm, left 2.5 cm, right 1.5 cm, bottom 1.0 cm. No frames around the usable surface.
- Lines: durable, black, sufficiently dense and dark, uniformly thick and well-defined, without colorings. Color drawings are not accepted.
- Text matter: only a single word or words when absolutely indispensable (examples: “water,” “steam,” “open,” “closed,” “section on AB,” or short catchwords in circuit/flow diagrams). Words must be placed so a translation can be pasted over without interfering with lines.
- Cross-sections: oblique hatching that does not impede reading of reference signs or lead lines.
- Scale: such that a photographic reduction to two-thirds still allows all details to be distinguished. Graphical scale preferred if scale is indicated.
- Reference signs: consistent throughout; signs not mentioned in the description must not appear in the drawings (and vice versa). Height of numbers/letters ≥ 0.32 cm. No brackets, circles, or inverted commas associated with numbers and letters.
- Figures numbered consecutively in Arabic numerals independently of sheet numbering. Several figures may appear on one sheet if clearly separated and without wasted space.
- Flow sheets and diagrams are considered drawings.

Drawings that comply with PCT Rule 11 must generally be accepted by designated Offices in the national phase; new drawings conforming only to national standards cannot be required if the PCT drawings already meet Rule 11.

## EPO (European Patent Office)
- Closely tracks PCT Rule 11 for formal requirements (A4, same core margins and usable area, black durable lines, limited text, character height ≥ 0.32 cm, hatching rules, scale allowing reduction to two-thirds).
- Since 1 October 2025 the EPO accepts color and greyscale drawings (and color photographs) in applications filed **electronically**. Other offices may reproduce them in black and white, so the description should make sense for both versions.
- This is EPO practice only. It does not change PCT Rule 11: the PCT international phase still requires black line drawings without colorings. For a Euro PCT route, prepare black and white drawings.
- Photographs may be accepted as drawings if they meet the formal criteria.
- No drawings in the request, description, claims, or abstract.
- Reference signs must correspond between drawings, description, and claims.

## Recommended “IP5-safe” / multi-office approach
To maximize acceptance across USPTO, PCT, EPO, and other major offices:
- Use **A4** paper size.
- Observe the stricter of the margin sets (top/left 2.5 cm, right ≥ 1.5–2.0 cm, bottom ≥ 1.0–2.0 cm depending on source; safest is to stay well inside 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.0 / 2.0 cm).
- Pure black solid lines, no color, minimal text (indispensable words only).
- Consistent reference numerals, proper hatching, scale that survives reduction to two-thirds.
- High-resolution vector or 600 dpi line art.
- No frames, no extraneous matter.

When the user indicates possible international filing, default to these stricter constraints rather than pure USPTO Letter-size practice. Color is not available under PCT; the EPO accepts it only for direct electronic EP filings.
